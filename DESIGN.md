## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, editorial luxury aesthetic tailored for high-fashion digital flagship retail. Balancing classic European couture traditions with razor-sharp modern minimalism, the aesthetic is deliberate, stark, and unapologetically refined. 

The visual style is rooted in **Minimalism** enriched by haute editorial layouts:
- Vast expanses of warm ivory and pure white canvas allow collection imagery to function as museum-grade focal points.
- Precision framing, hairline boundaries, and expansive whitespace echo print magazines and gallery monographs.
- The tone evokes prestige, exclusivity, confident restraint, and sensual sophistication. Signature burnt orange delivers an unexpected flash of modern avant-garde energy against disciplined monochrome surfaces.

## Colors

The palette adheres strictly to a classic couture ratio:
- **75% Base Canvas & Tonal Neutrals:** Warm Ivory (`#F8F5F0`), Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), and Champagne Beige (`#E9DFD2`). These provide a warm, tactile foundation that feels softer and more elevated than sterile pure digital white.
- **15% Structural Contrast:** Luxury Black (`#111111`) and Muted Charcoal (`#57534E`). Reserved for sharp typographic hierarchies, architectural divider rules, primary actions, and deep editorial framing.
- **10% Accent Energy:** Primary Burnt Orange (`#D35400`) and Accessible Dark Orange (`#B94700`). Applied with surgical precision to draw the eye to micro-interactions, selection indicators, high-priority notifications, and curated promotional accents without overwhelming the editorial stillness.

Accessible Dark Orange (`#B94700`) must be selected over `#D35400` whenever orange text or iconography rests directly upon Warm Ivory or Champagne Beige to preserve WCAG AA contrast compliance.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary elegance of classic serif editorial titles with the structured, contemporary clarity of a geometric sans-serif:
- **Display & Headlines (EB Garamond):** Evokes the lineage of French and Italian fashion houses. Renders collection titles, narrative storytelling, and editorial quotes with graceful, high-contrast strokes.
- **Functional UI & Product Details (Manrope):** Geometric, understated, and impeccably legible across small handheld viewports. Used for navigation, product titles, pricing, checkout flows, and system messaging.
- **Letter Spacing & Casing:** Category tags, section metadata, breadcrumbs, and primary CTA labels utilize uppercase transformation paired with generous letter spacing (`0.15em` to `0.2em`) to replicate engraved print luxury.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system employs a **mobile-first fluid grid** that translates into an expansive, asymmetric editorial spread on wider viewports:
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` outer canvas margin and `1rem` column gutters. Full-bleed edge-to-edge lookbooks pair with compact product details.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum width of `1600px` with `4rem` lateral margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Vertical sections leverage dramatic spacing (`space-2xl` and above) to simulate high-fashion printed publications.
- **Architectural Rhythms:** Negative space is treated as an active design material. Images are never crowded; editorial captions float adjacent to full-height collection photography rather than sitting pinned directly underneath.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system eschews soft skeuomorphism and heavy blurred drop shadows entirely, relying instead on **low-contrast outlines, architectural planar overlays, and tonal layering**:
- **Borders & Dividers:** Visual structure is established via 1px hairline rules using `#E9DFD2` against `#F8F5F0` or `#111111` at 10% opacity against pure white.
- **Sheet Overlays & Modals:** Drawer navigation, bag sheets, and quick-view panels use crisp background planes (`#FFFFFF` or `#F8F5F0`) bordered by hairline dividers. Overlays use a subdued luxury backdrop tint (`rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.45)`) without heavy gaussian blurs.
- **Hover & Active States:** Depth is conveyed through subtle tonal shifts (e.g., `#F8F5F0` darkening softly to `#E9DFD2`) and immediate line transformations rather than artificial Y-axis lift.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly **Sharp (`0`)**:
- All interactive controls, cards, input boundaries, imagery containers, and overlays feature `0px` border-radius.
- Crisp, 90-degree right angles emphasize bespoke tailoring, architectural grandeur, and structural purity.
- Pill shapes, rounded buttons, and soft corners are avoided to preserve the editorial, avant-garde character of the house.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Luxury Black (`#111111`) fill, Pure White text in `label-caps-lg`, uppercase, sharp corners, zero border. Height is 48px on mobile for optimal touch targets. Active/Hover: Background shifts to Accessible Dark Orange (`#B94700`).
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with a 1px solid Luxury Black (`#111111`) border. Text in `label-caps-lg` in `#111111`. Active/Hover: Inverts to solid `#111111` fill with Pure White text.
- **Tertiary / Text Action:** Borderless, 1px underline positioned 4px beneath uppercase `label-caps-sm` text. Accent hover transitions the underline color to Primary Burnt Orange (`#D35400`).

### Product Cards
- Clean, vertical orientation with no card borders and no drop shadows.
- Imagery dominates in an architectural 3:4 or 4:5 aspect ratio. Background of the image container rests on Warm Ivory (`#F8F5F0`).
- Information lockup beneath imagery: Brand or Sub-collection in `label-caps-sm` (`#57534E`), Product Title in `body-md` (`#111111`), and Price in `body-md` (`#111111`).
- Secondary hover/tap reveals alternate garment angle or editorial silhouette.

### Input Fields
- Understated architectural lines: 1px bottom border only (`#57534E` or `#E9DFD2`) or full 1px border box with 0px radius.
- Background remains transparent or Warm Ivory (`#F8F5F0`).
- Placeholder and label use `body-md` transitioning upward to `label-caps-sm` in Accessible Dark Orange (`#B94700`) upon focus.
- Focus state thickens the bottom border to 1.5px in Luxury Black (`#111111`) without outer glow rings.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** 18x18px square, 0px border radius, 1px border in Luxury Black (`#111111`). Checked state fills with Luxury Black (`#111111`) containing an architectural hairline white checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons / Swatches:** Color swatches display as sharp 24x24px squares framed by a 1px hairline border in `#E9DFD2`. Active swatch is framed by an outer 1px ring spaced 2px away in Luxury Black (`#111111`).

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Rectangular blocks with 0px radius. Transparent background with 1px border in `#E9DFD2`.
- Text styled in `label-caps-sm`.
- Active state uses solid Luxury Black (`#111111`) background with Pure White typography and an integrated close icon.

### Editorial Banners & Hero Modules
- Full-bleed vertical image framing on mobile, expanding to full-viewport split-screen layouts on desktop.
- Typographic overlays position Display headlines (`EB Garamond`) anchored to the bottom-left or centered with deliberate letter spacing and stark contrast.

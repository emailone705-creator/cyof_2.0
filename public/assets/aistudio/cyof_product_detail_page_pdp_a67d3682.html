Conceived at our historical Via della Spiga design studio, 'The Palazzo Sculptural Tote' synthesizes Renaissance architectural geometry with stark, contemporary volume. Each exemplar requires nineteen hours of dedicated assembly by master maroquiniers.

Reinforced through interior carbon-fiber gussets, the tote preserves its sculptural silhouette even when holding comprehensive business accoutrements. Finished with solid brass hardware hand-dipped in pale 24k satin gold.

  • Exterior: 100% Full-Grain Tuscan Calfskin Nappa, natural mineral vegetable tanning.
  • Lining: Sand-brushed split suede leather with interior calfskin trim.
  • Hardware: Solid architectural brass with satin pale-gold protective anodization.

Avoid prolonged exposure to direct midday sunlight and humid conditions. Nourish annually with CYOF Biological Leather Balm, supplied in your archival presentation chest.

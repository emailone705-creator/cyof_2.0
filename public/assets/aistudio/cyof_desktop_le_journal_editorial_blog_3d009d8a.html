An Architecture of Solitude and Grace

From the glacial meltwaters of the Piedmont foothills to the hand-stitched revers of our Paris atelier, the genesis of double-faced cashmere remains an exercise in rigorous quietude. Here, unhurried craftsmanship confronts contemporary structure.

In this monograph, we document the three-month journey of rare vicuña and superfine Merino, woven on vintage projectile looms that whisper with the cadence of 19th-century Piedmont.

Hélène Delacroix
